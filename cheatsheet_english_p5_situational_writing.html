📗 English Cheat Sheet

P5 Situational Writing

The 2025 syllabus changes, the PAC framework, format templates, and a strict 20-minute exam strategy — everything for a near-perfect score.

🆕1. The 2025 Game Changers

If you practice older past-year papers, you'll notice three massive shifts implemented in the 2025 papers:

The 14-Mark Crunch

Total marks dropped from 15 to 14. Content is still worth 6 marks, but Language and Organisation has been reduced from 9 to 8 marks. Takeaway: with fewer language marks, every grammatical slip, spelling mistake, or punctuation error cuts deeper into your score.

The Underlined Point (Inference)

Look closely at the Task Box in any 2025 paper — one bullet point is completely underlined. This is your inference point: an idea you must generate using your own critical thinking. You will not find the answer in the visual graphics or text — you have to invent a logical, realistic reason yourself.

The Brand-New "Article" Format

Alongside emails, letters, and reports, you can now be asked to write a newsletter, blog post, or magazine article for your school population.

🥇2. The Golden Framework: PAC

Before your pen even touches the paper, spend 2 minutes scanning the prompt to extract your PAC:

P

Purpose — Why are you writing? Look for action words in the task like to invite, to complain, to inform, to praise, or to report. Start your introduction by stating this clearly.

A

Audience — Who is reading this? Writing to a friend (informal) requires a completely different tone than writing to your Principal or a Centre Manager (formal).

C

Context — What is the setting? Are you a student leader writing an article for the school website? A helpful cousin sending an email? The context dictates your greeting and how you introduce yourself.

🧱4. Step-by-Step Layout Templates

The New 2025 Article Format

[Insert a Catchy, Relevant Title] By [Your Full Name, e.g., Lucas Tan] Dear Students, I am Lucas Tan from Primary 5 Resilience, and I am excited to share my recent experience at the Environment Club's annual green workshop... [Body Paragraph 1: Cover the first 2-3 content points from the stimulus] [Body Paragraph 2: Cover the remaining points + the Underlined Inference Point] Thank you for reading, and let us all do our part to keep our school clean!

✅5. The "6/6 Content" Checklist

To guarantee full marks for Task Fulfilment, use the Number & Highlight technique:

  1. Go to the Task Box and physically number the bullet points 1 to 6.
  2. Scan the visual stimulus and highlight the exact phrase that answers each bullet point.
  3. Attack the Underlined Point: when addressing the underlined bullet point, do not use generic fluff. Make it hyper-specific to the context.

Bad Inference: "I think you will like this activity because it is fun."

Good 2025 Paper Inference: "I highly recommend the clay-making workshop for Miss Huang because she loves arts and crafts, and she frequently creates beautiful pottery during her weekends."

⏱️6. The 20-Minute Exam Strategy

Situational writing is worth a significant chunk of your Paper 1 marks. Do not rush it at the end. Use this strict time breakdown:

  1. The 3-Minute PAC & Plan (Minutes 0–3): identify the format, determine if the register is formal or informal, and number all 6 content points. Brainstorm your original idea for the underlined inference point.
  2. The 14-Minute Execution (Minutes 3–17): write out the format frames carefully (addresses, dates, subject lines). Draft the paragraphs. Keep sentences clear and concise rather than overly complex.
  3. The 3-Minute Proofreading Scan (Minutes 17–20): check for the big three killers — Subject-Verb Agreement (e.g., every student has vs all students have), Tense Consistency, and Punctuation (especially missing commas after salutations or full stops at the end of sign-offs).
Pro-Tip from the 2025 Top-School Answer Keys: Examiners are actively penalising students who copy entire chunks of text word-for-word from the stimulus. Show off your language skills by using synonyms and rephrasing the given points naturally while preserving the exact facts!
